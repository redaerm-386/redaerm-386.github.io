Privacy policy

This application is a private Home Assistant installation used only by its owner to control the owner's own Google Nest devices.

  • Data accessed (thermostat status and settings) is used only to display and control those devices within the owner's home.
  • Data stays on the owner's local Home Assistant server and is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data from other people is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account settings.
